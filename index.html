About This Cookbook

Europe's Best-Kept Culinary Secret

Croatian cuisine is a magnificent tapestry — Mediterranean simplicity on the coast, Central European richness inland, and centuries of Venetian, Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian influence woven through every dish.

This cookbook gathers Croatia's most beloved recipes — the ones that appear at village weddings, Christmas tables, and Sunday lunches that last until dusk. Every recipe includes phonetic pronunciation, exact measurements, full nutrition info, chef's tips, and the traditions that give Croatian food its meaning.
